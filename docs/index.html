Features

Built for people who take
their money seriously.

Every feature is intentional. Nothing is bloat. This is what a well-engineered personal finance app looks like.

📊
Animated Balance Counter
Your balance ticks up and down — 600ms smooth counter — every time a transaction is saved. You physically feel the money move. No other free app does this.
// AnimatedBalance — value counts to new amount
Animated.timing(animVal, {
  toValue: 42580,
  duration: 600,
  useNativeDriver: false
}).start();
🛡️
Financial Health Score
A real 0–100 score built on savings rate, budget adherence, balance, and income logging — not a vanity metric.
🔥
Under-Budget Streak
Stay under your daily budget and build a streak. 7 days = ⚡, 14 days = 🔥. Oddly addictive.
🧪
What-If Simulator
Nudge each spending category and instantly see how much you'd save per year. Real-time projection, no other free app has this.
🔔
9pm Day in Review
A nightly local notification: "₹840 spent. ₹160 under budget 🔥". No server. Pure on-device scheduling.
🏆
Goal Confetti Celebration
Hit a savings goal and get a full-screen 22-particle confetti animation with haptic feedback. The most emotional moment in any finance app — engineered to make users send screenshots to friends.
🎊 Particle engine
📳 Haptic feedback
🎯 Per-goal savings
🗂
Per-Category Budgets
Set monthly limits per category. Color-coded progress bars warn you before you overspend on Food, Shopping, Travel, and more.
✏️
Edit & Back-Date
Long-press any transaction to edit. Forgot to log rent on the 1st? Back-date it with the built-in date picker. A finance app that trusts you.
🔐
PIN + Biometric
4-digit PIN with fingerprint/face unlock. Your data stays private even if someone picks up your phone.
🔁
Recurring Tags
Mark transactions as daily, weekly, or monthly. EMIs, subscriptions, rent — always visible at a glance.
🧠
Smart Insights
Auto-detects peak spending days, weekend patterns, and low income-logging. Surfaces actionable observations, not noise.

Getting Started

Up and running in 60 seconds.

No sign-up. No form filling. Install and start. It's that simple.

01
Download the APK
Grab the latest release from GitHub Releases. Sideload on any Android phone — no Play Store account needed.
02
Set your budget
One question on first launch: your monthly budget. Pick a preset or type your own. That's the entire setup.
03
Log your income
Add your salary. Your balance comes alive. The app is now personally yours with real numbers, not zeros.
04
Build the streak
Log expenses daily. Stay under budget. The 9pm notification becomes your money ritual. Watch the streak grow.
