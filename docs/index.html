Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built around the features that actually change how you think about money — not just track it.

📊
Animated Balance Counter
Your balance counts up and down every time a transaction is added. You feel the money move. That's the whole point.
🔥
Under-Budget Streak
Stay under your daily budget and build a streak. 7 days gets you a ⚡, 14 days gets you 🔥. It's oddly addictive.
🏆
Goal Confetti
Hit a savings goal and get a full-screen confetti celebration. The most emotional moment in any finance app — and we nailed it.
🧪
What-If Simulator
Drag a slider to cut your Food budget by ₹1,000 and instantly see how much you'd save per year. No other free app does this.
🔔
9pm Day in Review
"Today: ₹840 spent. You're ₹160 under budget 🔥" — a nightly local notification that builds the daily habit. No server needed.
🗂
Category Budgets
Set monthly limits per category — ₹3k for Food, ₹2k for Entertainment. Color-coded progress bars warn you before you overspend.
✏️
Edit & Back-Date
Entered the wrong amount? Long-press to edit. Forgot to log rent on the 1st? Back-date it. Finally, a finance app that trusts you.
🔐
PIN & Biometric Lock
Protect your finances with a 4-digit PIN or fingerprint/face unlock. Because your money data deserves real security.
🔁
Recurring Transactions
Tag transactions as daily, weekly, or monthly. EMI, Netflix, rent — marked clearly so you never lose track of fixed costs.

Getting Started
